If symptoms are happening right now, stop and call emergency services. Sudden face drooping, arm weakness or speech difficulty — even if it passes — is an emergency. This tool estimates risk over years, not what is happening today.

Pulse rhythm check

Your camera measures the pulse in your fingertip for one minute and checks whether the rhythm is regular. This can rule atrial fibrillation out of your estimate — it cannot rule it in.

60s
Cover the rear camera and flash with your fingertip.
  • Use the back camera. Rest the pad of your finger over both the lens and the flash.
  • Press gently — hard pressure cuts off the blood flow being measured.
  • Sit still and rest your hand on a table. Movement is the main cause of a failed reading.
  • If your phone refuses camera access, or it isn’t working, just skip — the rest still works.
