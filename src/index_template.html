Stroke Risk Self-Assessment

This page answers a different question from the 5-year risk calculator. It estimates how strongly a set of self-reported characteristics is associated with having already had a stroke, using a machine learning model trained on 253,680 real CDC survey respondents and benchmarked against classical clinical risk scores. Every question below is something you can answer about yourself — no clinic visit, lab test, or imaging required.

This is a self-assessment research tool, not a diagnostic device. It does not replace medical advice. If you are experiencing stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), call emergency services immediately.

About this estimate: Generated entirely in your browser — no data was sent anywhere. Model: XGBoost, isotonic-calibrated, trained and validated on CDC BRFSS 2015 (n=253,680). Test-set performance: AUROC 0.828 (95% CI 0.819–0.836) vs. 0.789 for the classical-score baseline shown above, a statistically significant improvement. This tool reflects a cross-sectional self-report association, not a clinically confirmed prospective risk calculation — see the accompanying paper for full methodology and limitations.
