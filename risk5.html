5-Year Stroke Risk

Your estimated probability of having a first stroke within the next five years, from a published survival model built on 23,691 adults followed prospectively with physician-adjudicated stroke events. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

If symptoms are happening right now, stop and call emergency services. Sudden face drooping, arm weakness, or speech difficulty — even if it passes — is a medical emergency. This page estimates risk over years. It cannot tell you anything about what is happening today.
This is not a diagnosis. It is a statistical estimate for a group of people who share your answers, not a prediction about you personally. It does not replace a clinician, and no decision about medication should be made from this number.
